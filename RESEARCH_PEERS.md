# JANSSON — peer teardown, 10 solo video-creator portfolios

_Researched 2026-09-18._ The v3 brief's ten references were studios, agencies and
inspiration platforms (BUCK, Locomotive, Obys, Resn, Cosmos, Are.na). Only Sam
Kolder was a true peer. This round covers **solo video creators only** — the
actual competitive set.

## Answers to the open decisions

### 1. Does anyone group work by client? — Yes, but only the closest peer
**Sam Kolder groups by client/brand**: Insta360, DJI, Personal. Every other
portfolio-first peer groups by **role** (Willner, Heimir) or **format/service**
(Levin, Mably, Cussens). Client grouping is rare but validated by the one site
that most resembles JANSSON.

Two details worth copying. Kolder carries the client as a **label on the card
inside one run**, not as separate headed sub-sections — it scales when a client has
one video. And he has a **"Personal" bucket**, which is exactly JANSSON's "Other".
An unlabelled bucket is the norm, not a failure state.

### 2. Is a cap of 4 defensible? — Yes, at the tight end of normal
Peer caps: Kolder ~4 · Maragos 6 · Cussens 6 then load-more · Willner ~6–7 ·
Mably ~8 · Heimir ~12 (the outlier that shows everything). Median ≈ 6.

Industry guidance is tighter still: a video-editor portfolio should hold **8–12
pieces total**, and **reviewers typically watch only the first 3–5 before forming a
judgement**. General portfolio advice lands on 2–5 curated pieces.

JANSSON at 4 per group × 3 groups = **12 visible on load**, which sits exactly in the
8–12 band while keeping all 24 reachable. Defensible as-is.

### 3. Was removing the standalone showreel right? — The section, yes. The link, no.
Almost nobody gives the reel a full-height destination section: Kolder keeps an
**icon in the hero**, Willner a **featured item above the grid**, Levin a homepage
**section**, Cussens an **embedded player**, Mably a de-emphasised link. Only
Heimir, Maragos and Goode have no reel entry at all.

So: killing the CRT-TV section matches peers. But **6 of 10 keep a reel reachable
from the top of the page**, and JANSSON currently has none — the section is parked
*and* the "▶ Showreel" button was removed from the landing, so there is no entry
point anywhere. That is the one place this teardown contradicts the earlier call.

### 4. CTA buttons under the wordmark? — Zero or one, never two
Portfolio-first peers run **no CTA** (Mably, Heimir, Maragos) or **exactly one**
(Willner → contact, Levin → email, Kolder → email + reel icon). Multiple CTAs appear
only on lead-gen businesses: Cussens ("book a consultation", WhatsApp, Calendly),
Haapoja (3 product cards), D'Avella (newsletter). Removing the gold pill pair was
right. A single quiet contact or reel affordance would still be within the norm.

### 5. Is "Editing / Films / Social" the right vocabulary? — The weakest link
Peers hold **one axis** and stay on it:
- all **role** — Cinematographer, Director, Producer, Editor, VFX
- all **format** — Commercial+Brand, Documentary, Narrative+Music Video, Automotive
- all **service** — Commercials, Corporate, Documentary
- all **client** — Insta360, DJI, Personal

JANSSON currently mixes three axes on one row: **Editing** is a role, **Films** is a
format, **Social** is a distribution channel. No peer does this. A viewer can't tell
what question the row is answering.

Fix by picking one axis:
- format — **Films / Short Form / Social**
- role — **Directing / Editing / Camera**

Note also that no traditional-film peer uses "Social" at top level — but none of them
are social-first creators, and JANSSON is. Keeping Social is right; the inconsistency
is pairing it with a role word.

## Where JANSSON is already ahead

1. **Motion in the grid.** Only Kolder and Cussens play video in the index. Mably,
   Willner and Heimir still use static thumbnails. The hover-to-play grid plus the
   always-on loops on the landing put JANSSON ahead of most of the set.
2. **The landing.** No peer has anything as distinctive as the parallaxed field of
   work around a wordmark. Most open on a hero image and a role line.
3. **Engineering.** Self-hosted fonts, no trackers, no build step, one file. The
   WordPress/Squarespace cohort carries far more weight for less.
4. **Side projects on-domain is validated.** Kolder (shop, masterclass), Haapoja
   (three businesses), D'Avella (courses), Willner (photography) all keep non-client
   work on the same domain. Lab / Rugs / Königskarten belong where they are.

## Recommended, in priority order

1. **Restore a reel entry point.** Not the CRT section — a link or small embed near
   the top, the way 6 of 10 peers do it.
2. **Fix the taxonomy axis.** "Films / Short Form / Social" or "Directing / Editing /
   Camera". Not a mix.
3. **Reconsider the Social sub-headings.** Kolder's pattern — client as a card label
   in one run — scales better than headed sub-sections holding one clip each.
   Revisit once the `client` fields are filled in and the real shape is visible.
4. **Keep the cap at 4.** Backed by both the peer median and the 8–12 guidance.
5. **Leave the CTA count at zero or one.** Do not reintroduce a pair.
